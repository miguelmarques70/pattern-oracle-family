# Pattern Oracle Identity

**Pattern Oracle Family**\
**Author and steward:** Miguel Marques\
**Proposed edition:** Version 1 — October 2026\
**Status:** Draft for Author Seat adoption; not yet an adopted policy.

**Publication link:** [https://github.com/miguelmarques70/pattern-oracle-family](https://github.com/miguelmarques70/pattern-oracle-family)

## 1. Purpose

Pattern Oracle Identity identifies the original Pattern Oracle Family, its member Oracles and its authored publications. It gives readers a way to distinguish the original work, its official editions, its historical material and independent developments inspired by it.

Identity preserves provenance while allowing development. It does not make the Family a spiritual authority over the people who encounter it, or require other symbolic systems to adopt its architecture.

## 2. The original Family

The Pattern Oracle Family is authored and stewarded by Miguel Marques. Its shared architecture is presented in the Pattern Oracle Family Manual. Its four original member Oracles are:

| **Oracle**        | **Principal field of inquiry**                                             | **Companion publications**                                    |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Oracle of Density | Density, polarity, catalyst and soul evolution                             | Oracle of Density Manual; Oracle of Density: Origins and Code |
| Contract          | Vows, agreement-patterns, soul-contract echoes and spiritual agency        | Contract Manual; Contract: Origins and Code                   |
| Lineage           | Ancestral patterns, inherited relations, reincarnational motifs and repair | Lineage Manual; Lineage: Origins and Code                     |
| Collective Oracle | Group relations, social memory, collective polarity and planetary inquiry  | Collective Oracle Manual; Collective Oracle: Origins and Code |

These fields describe each Oracle's orientation. They do not confer privileged access to another person's inner life, an absent group's consent, or independently verified metaphysical facts.

The Manual and Origins and Code are companion publications of the same Oracle. Distribution as separate books or files does not create separate Oracle identities. The Family Manual supplies the shared architecture; it is not an additional Oracle member.

## 3. Authorship and assistance

Miguel Marques retains the Author Seat for the original Family. GPT assistance, editorial contributions, facilitation and implementation should be acknowledged according to their actual roles. Their participation does not independently authorize publication as an official edition or adoption into the original canon.

Newly generated text remains a proposal until adopted by the Author. Assistance does not establish the provenance of every phrase or grant rights over another person's material.

## 4. Continuity and developmental history

The Family may develop without rewriting its earlier forms as though later distinctions had always existed. Historical material, mature Codes, operational prompts and subsequent additions should remain identifiable by their source status and edition.

In the Oracle of Density, the Historical Density Code and Stable Density Code are distinct developmental forms of the same Oracle. Across the Family, historical interpretations and current operational instructions should not be presented as interchangeable sources.

An edition may retain historical wording while documenting changes to presentation. A later completion or interpretation should be identified as an addition rather than silently described as recovered historical text.

## 5. Official identity and independent work

Official status arises through an explicit designation by Miguel Marques under the Canonical Designation Policy. Compatibility, resemblance, use of a published prompt, or circulation among readers does not alone establish that designation.

Independent adaptations may identify their relationship to the original Family and provide accurate attribution. Their own title, creator, version and independent status should be clear wherever official identity could otherwise be misunderstood.

An independent creator may develop a different interpretation, method, architecture or Oracle within the applicable permissions. Such work does not require canonical approval merely because it develops from published Family material. It may establish its own identity and internal canon without becoming an official member of the original Family.

A copy or adaptation should not claim an endorsement or official designation that has not been granted. Accurate references to the original work are compatible with maintaining an independent identity.

## 6. Relationship to licensing

This document describes identity and official designation; it does not amend CC BY-NC 4.0 or impose an additional approval requirement on uses that licence permits. Commercial adaptation is addressed separately in Open Access, Living Canon, and Independent Adaptation. No new commercial permission is created here.

Third-party rights remain separate. Identity or canonical designation does not establish permission to reproduce external material. Nothing here declares the Family's names registered trademarks or determines rights not established by the applicable law.

## 7. Identification of editions

An official publication should identify its title, Oracle where applicable, author, publication version, publication date and designation record. If it includes an operational prompt, its prompt version should be stated separately from the publication version.

Historical status, adaptations, material additions and editorial changes should be disclosed where relevant. Public references should identify the actual edition rather than imply that a filename, an AI output or an unspecified latest version is authoritative.

## 8. Adoption and revision

This draft takes effect only through Miguel Marques's explicit adoption and publication as a policy. Its adopted version and date should then be recorded. Later revisions should remain distinguishable from earlier editions.

The Human retains the Author Seat. Readers and independent creators retain their own discernment, agency and responsibility.
