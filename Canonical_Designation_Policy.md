# Canonical Designation Policy

**Pattern Oracle Family**\
**Author and steward:** Miguel Marques\
**Proposed edition:** Version 1 — October 2026\
**Status:** Draft for Author Seat adoption; not yet an adopted policy.

**Publication link:** [https://github.com/miguelmarques70/pattern-oracle-family](https://github.com/miguelmarques70/pattern-oracle-family)

## 1. Purpose and meaning

This policy describes how Miguel Marques designates editions, materials and implementations as official parts of the original Pattern Oracle Family.

“Canonical” means explicitly adopted into that authored body of work. It does not mean infallible, complete, scientifically verified, spiritually binding or beyond criticism. A canonical edition may contain historical material, unresolved provenance or documented gaps; those limitations remain visible.

## 2. The designation authority

Miguel Marques retains the Author Seat and the authority to designate the original Family's official works. An editor, AI system, facilitator, publisher, contributor or independent developer cannot grant that status merely through use, compatibility or a claim of continuity.

Contributions become part of the original canon through explicit author adoption, with their actual authorship, source status and necessary permissions recognized. Submission creates no obligation to accept or incorporate a contribution.

## 3. What a designation covers

A designation identifies a particular publication, edition, component or implementation. Its scope should be stated rather than inferred.

Adopting one book does not automatically adopt unpublished drafts, future revisions, every AI-generated reading or an independently hosted implementation. A prompt's designation does not certify every output produced through it. An official implementation should have its own identifiable release and scope of adoption.

Publication versions and operational-prompt versions are separate records. An edition identified as Version 1 can contain Operational Prompt Version 1.4 without contradiction.

## 4. The designation record

An adopted record should state the exact title, Oracle or shared Family role, publication version, adoption date, identifier, designated status and scope. Where relevant, it should also state the publication date, included prompt version and material changes from the previous edition.

The record may appear in the publication's front notice and in an author-issued release list. A separate external registry is not required by this policy. The identifier serves traceability; it does not by itself prove adoption.

## 5. Historical material and later additions

Historical material can be adopted as part of an official edition without being presented as current operational instruction or verified fact. Its developmental position should remain clear.

Recovered material, mechanical corrections, layout changes, substantive revisions and newly drafted additions should be distinguished in the edition record or editorial notes as appropriate. Missing material should not be represented as recovered merely because a later writer supplies a plausible completion.

New interpretations generated during consultation or editorial work remain proposals until explicitly adopted. Adoption acknowledges their later origin; it does not retrospectively make them historical source text.

## 6. Independent adaptations

Canonical approval is not a condition for noncommercial adaptations permitted by CC BY-NC 4.0. Qualifying commercial adaptations are governed by the separate Independent Commercial Adaptation Permission, not by a requirement to join the original canon.

Independent creators may maintain their own identity, editions and canon within the applicable permissions. Compatibility with Family materials does not confer official status or endorsement. Attribution should not be described as author approval.

This policy does not modify the standard CC licence, create additional copyright restrictions on licensed reuse or extend the Author's permissions to third-party material. Official designation and reproduction rights remain separate questions.

## 7. Corrections and changes of status

The Author may correct or revise an edition and may designate a release as draft, adopted, superseded, suspended, retired or withdrawn. These statuses describe stewardship of the original Family and its current recommendations.

A status change should identify the affected edition, date, reason and replacement where applicable. Superseding a release does not erase its historical existence or make a later version identical to it.

A change of official status does not itself revoke permissions already granted under CC BY-NC 4.0. Any separate permission is governed by its own terms. The record should distinguish changing recommendations from changing legal permissions.

## 8. Conflicts and unavailable material

Identify the relevant edition and the nature of a conflict before claiming agreement. If an adoption record or source cannot be established, mark the status unresolved and seek clarification rather than inventing canonical standing.

This policy governs designation. Pattern Oracle Identity identifies the authored Family; the Family Manual presents shared architecture; specialist Manuals, Codes and prompts carry their stated functions. This policy does not create a blanket precedence rule among their interpretations or claim that an Oracle Family Constitution already exists.

## 9. Adoption of this policy

This draft takes effect only when Miguel Marques explicitly adopts it and records the adopted version and date. Publication of the adopted policy should make its current text accessible alongside the Family's other companion documents.

The Human retains the Author Seat. Canonical designation preserves an identifiable authored work while leaving independent development open within the applicable permissions.
