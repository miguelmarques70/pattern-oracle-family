# Open Access, Living Canon, and Independent Adaptation

**Pattern Oracle Family — Public Release and Licensing Framework**

**Author:** Miguel Marques\
**Version:** 1\
**Proposed publication date:** October 2026\
**Effective date:** Upon explicit author adoption and public release\
**Status:** Revised draft for Author Seat adoption; legal review remains pending

**Publication link:** [https://github.com/miguelmarques70/pattern-oracle-family](https://github.com/miguelmarques70/pattern-oracle-family)

## Preamble

The Pattern Oracle Family is made available to support study, exploration, interpretation and independent creative development.

Its original published works are freely accessible for non-commercial use. Readers may share, translate, revise, expand and adapt them under the permissions established in this framework. Independent creators may also develop and commercialise genuinely adapted works under their own clearly distinguishable names and identities.

The original canon remains living. The author may correct, revise, expand and publish future canonical editions, including editions that incorporate community contributions with appropriate permission.

Access does not require agreement with the original interpretations. Adaptation does not confer official identity. Canonical recognition does not transfer ownership of another person's work.

This framework preserves these distinctions while establishing a common publication and licensing structure for the Pattern Oracle Family.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This document governs the publication, access and permitted reuse of original Pattern Oracle materials expressly released under it.

The Pattern Oracle Family is authored and stewarded by Miguel Marques. Its shared architecture is presented in the Pattern Oracle Family Manual. Its four original member Oracles are the Oracle of Density, Contract, Lineage and Collective Oracle.

A publication is covered only when its publication record expressly identifies this framework and the applicable licence.

Unpublished manuscripts, private working drafts, unreleased operational prompts, internal development materials and other unpublished works are not automatically covered.

## 2. Uniform licensing across publication formats

The same licensing terms apply to all original Pattern Oracle materials expressly released for public use, regardless of publication format.

These include public-facing manuals, technical Code Books, published operational prompts and other designated original texts.

Each publication shall carry a publication and licence notice identifying its title, author, version, publication date, applicable licence and any additional permissions.

The distinction between a Manual, a Code Book and an operational prompt concerns their function, not their basic reuse permissions. A Manual and its companion Origins and Code volume belong to the same Oracle identity even when distributed separately. Publication versions and operational-prompt versions are identified separately.

Materials incorporated from third parties remain subject to their own rights and are not relicensed by this framework.

## 4. Additional independent commercial-adaptation permission

In addition to CC BY-NC 4.0, Miguel Marques grants the following permission for original material expressly released under this framework.

### 4.1 Permission granted

Any person or organisation may create, reproduce, distribute and commercialise a genuinely adapted work derived from covered Pattern Oracle material without requesting individual commercial authorisation, provided that all conditions in this section are satisfied.

This permission is worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free and irrevocable, provided that its conditions continue to be met. 

It applies only to material for which Miguel Marques has the authority to grant the relevant rights.

### 4.2 Genuine adaptation

A qualifying commercial adaptation must contain substantive original development beyond the reproduction or presentation of the source material.

Such development may include substantial new writing, interpretation, architecture, educational material, implementation, translation combined with substantial original development, or another meaningful transformation.

For purposes of this additional commercial permission, incorporation into AI or AGI systems—including internal prompts, retrieval collections and other components not visible to users—does not, by itself, constitute genuine adaptation. A commercial application relying on this permission must include substantive original development and satisfy the attribution, change-disclosure and independent-identity conditions of this section. Where source material is not visible to users, the required attribution and disclosures must remain accessible in the application’s documentation or credits. 

Renaming, reformatting, changing the cover, making minor editorial changes or repackaging substantially unchanged source material does not, by itself, qualify.

Whether a particular work qualifies depends on the substance of the adaptation, not merely on how its creator describes it.

### 4.3 Independent identity

A commercial adaptation must have a clearly distinguishable name and presentation.

Its creator must not represent it as an original Pattern Oracle, an official edition, an authorised successor, an endorsed implementation or part of the original canon without separate authorisation.

Accurate identification of the source work for attribution, scholarship, commentary or explanation remains permitted.

A minor rearrangement or variation of an original Oracle’s name does not, by itself, establish an independent identity. The adaptation’s name and presentation must clearly distinguish it from the original work and avoid implying official status or endorsement. 

This permission does not grant trademark rights or exclusive rights in the original oracle identities.

### 4.4 Attribution and change disclosure

A commercially distributed adaptation must identify Miguel Marques as the author of the original source material, name the source work and identify its source edition where reasonably available.

It must state that the work is an independent adaptation and indicate that changes have been made.

Attribution must not imply endorsement or official collaboration.

### 4.5 Rights in independent contributions

Creators retain their rights in their own original contributions, subject to the rights and permissions applicable to the source material.

They may establish their own licensing terms for their original contributions, subject to the rights and permissions applicable to the source material. Those terms do not alter the CC BY-NC 4.0 permissions attached to identifiable source material already released under that licence. The licence on the source does not automatically place a creator’s new contributions under the same licence.

An independent adaptation may establish its own version history, governance arrangements and internal canon.

### 4.6 Uses requiring separate authorisation

This additional permission does not authorise:

- Commercial republication of substantially unchanged original Pattern Oracle materials.
- Commercial operation or distribution under the original oracle identities in a manner implying official status.
- Use of third-party material beyond the rights independently available for that material.
- Representations of authorisation, endorsement or canonical designation that have not been granted.

Other uses may be permitted by applicable law or by a separate written agreement.

### 4.7 Relationship to CC BY-NC 4.0

This commercial-adaptation permission is a separate grant. It does not replace, amend or reduce the permissions granted under CC BY-NC 4.0.

A recipient may rely on CC BY-NC 4.0 for uses within that licence, or on this additional permission for qualifying commercial adaptations.

The conditions of this additional permission apply to uses made in reliance upon it.

## 5. Open access

Original published Pattern Oracle materials shall be made available for reading and study without charge through the author's designated public distribution channels.

Readers may share and adapt released material for non-commercial purposes under CC BY-NC 4.0.

Free access does not require community membership, agreement with the author's interpretations or an ongoing relationship with the author.

Publication does not create an obligation to provide individual readings, support, updates or indefinite maintenance.

## 6. Independent adaptation

Independent creators may develop alternative interpretations, architectures, educational resources, translations and implementations in accordance with the applicable permissions.

They are not required to preserve every original formulation or follow subsequent canonical revisions.

Independent adaptations may develop their own identities and internal canons. Canonical approval is not required for uses permitted by CC BY-NC 4.0 or for commercial adaptations that satisfy Section 4. Independent status should be clear wherever official identity could otherwise be misunderstood; no adaptation acquires official status merely through compatibility with the original works. This distinction does not impose additional conditions on the standard CC licence.

## 7. Pattern Oracle identity

The identity of the original Pattern Oracle Family and its constituent oracles is addressed in the separate **Pattern Oracle Identity** document.

That document identifies the original Family, its member Oracles and its companion publications, and distinguishes official editions from independent adaptations. It addresses identity and provenance without adding an approval requirement to licensed reuse.

This framework does not claim ownership of general ideas, methods, traditions or other material that applicable law leaves available for independent use.

Copyright permissions, official identity and canonical designation are separate matters.

## 8. The living canon

The original Pattern Oracle canon remains open to correction, revision and development.

Miguel Marques retains authority to designate future canonical editions of the original works, subject to any express and documented delegation or succession arrangement.

A new edition may revise, expand, reorganise or replace material from an earlier edition.

Canonical adoption requires an explicit act by Miguel Marques under the **Canonical Designation Policy**, identifying the particular edition, material or implementation adopted. A contribution or adaptation does not become canonical through publication, popularity, recurrence or technical compatibility alone. AI-generated additions remain proposals until adopted. Canonical status identifies a place in the authored body of work; it does not establish infallibility, completeness, scientific verification or spiritual authority over readers.

Historical material, mature Codes, operational prompts and later additions should remain distinguishable. Newly drafted completions must not be described as recovered historical source text. Adoption of a later addition does not change its origin.

New canonical editions do not automatically invalidate independent adaptations developed from earlier releases.

## 9. Community contributions

Readers and independent creators may propose corrections, interpretations, extensions and other contributions for consideration.

Submission does not guarantee review, acceptance, incorporation or canonical status.

Contributors retain rights in their original material unless otherwise agreed. Copyright-protected contributions may be incorporated into future canonical releases only where an appropriate licence or separate permission exists.

Where a formal submission process is established, its contribution terms should be provided before submission, identifying the permissions requested and the agreed attribution. Until such terms are published and adopted, no transfer of rights or permission to incorporate a contribution should be inferred merely from its submission; incorporation requires an applicable licence or separately recorded permission.

Independent contributors remain free to develop their own works, subject to any applicable agreements.

## 10. Canonical designation and release records

Every official public release should have a publication record identifying its title, author, Oracle or shared Family role, edition, publication version, publication date, applicable licence, additional permissions, designation status and scope. Where a prompt is included, its version should be recorded separately. An adopted release should identify its designation record and adoption date. The record may appear in the publication and an author-issued release list; this framework does not require a separate external registry.

Canonical status is governed by the **Canonical Designation Policy**.

A release may be identified as draft, adopted, superseded, suspended, retired or withdrawn. The current adopted edition should be identifiable. Historical editions may remain archived with a record of their status, dates and material changes. A status change does not erase their history or automatically adopt a replacement.

A change of official status does not itself revoke permissions already granted under CC BY-NC 4.0. Any separate permission remains governed by its own terms. Changes of designation or recommendation should be distinguished from changes to legal permissions.

## 11. Third-party sources and protected expression

This framework licenses only material that its rights holder is authorised to license.

The Pattern Oracle Family may acknowledge conceptual inspiration from the I Ching, the Law of One / Ra Material and other traditions or published works. Such acknowledgment does not imply ownership of those sources or authorisation from their rights holders.

Original explanations, interpretations, arrangements and technical writing may be released under this framework where the author holds the necessary rights.

Third-party quotations, illustrations, substantial borrowed passages and other protected material must be identified where applicable. Their use remains subject to the relevant rights, permissions and legal exceptions.

A public manual, Code Book or operational prompt must not imply that this licence authorises reproduction of third-party material beyond the rights actually available.

Acknowledgment of GPT assistance does not establish that every supplied phrase originated with GPT or identify its external sources. Where the provenance of traditional line phrases or English renderings remains unknown, that uncertainty should be disclosed without inventing a translation credit. Disclosure records the uncertainty; it does not establish reproduction rights.

## 12. Publication, revision and continuity

The author may publish corrections, revised editions and new materials.

Each new publication shall identify the terms applicable to that release. Future editions may have different terms where the author has the necessary rights to apply them.

Revisions to this framework shall be identified by version and effective date.

The author may continue, pause or conclude development of an oracle. Publication does not create an obligation to maintain the work indefinitely.

Rights already validly granted under an irrevocable licence remain governed by that licence.

## 13. Relationship to companion documents

This framework operates alongside:

**Pattern Oracle Identity**, which addresses the identifiable continuity and representation of the original oracle family.

**Canonical Designation Policy**, which governs explicit adoption, the scope of designation, release records and changes of official status. Any delegation or succession arrangement must be expressly documented; it is not created by these companion documents.

Separate contribution terms may be published if a formal submission process is established. This framework does not represent such a document as already existing.

The publication record for each work should identify the applicable adopted versions of the companion documents. An unpublished draft is not an effective governing policy merely because another document cites its title. When online access becomes available, references should give the actual public destination.

These documents have different functions. A copyright permission does not confer canonical standing, and canonical standing does not automatically grant rights in another person's protected material.

## 14. Publication and licence notice

Every publicly released Manual, Code Book and operational prompt shall carry a short publication and licence notice consistent with this framework. Templates may be supplied for convenience; the absence of a separate template does not change the permissions stated here.

The notice shall identify the specific publication and refer readers to this document and the complete applicable licence.

The full licensing framework need not be reproduced inside every oracle publication.
